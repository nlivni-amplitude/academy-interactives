Both platforms carry the same statistical toolbox. The difference that trips people up is the default: Amplitude runs sequential testing out of the box so you can peek anytime, while Statsig defaults to fixed-horizon frequentist and offers sequential, SPRT, and Bayesian as opt-in modes.

Note
The concepts below are the same math on both sides. What changes is which one is on by default and how visibly it's surfaced. Knowing that lets you answer "does Statsig have X" without hedging: usually yes, the real question is whether it's the default or a toggle.
