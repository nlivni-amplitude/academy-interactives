Explained in non-technical terms
It's how long you'd stand at a crosswalk counting cars to be sure one color is more common. A big, obvious gap you'd spot in a few minutes. A tiny one takes all afternoon, and on a quiet road it might take days. Smaller effects and thinner traffic both mean more waiting.

The smaller the effect you want to catch, the more users you need to tell it apart from noise, and that number climbs sharply as the effect shrinks. Divide it by your daily traffic and you get days. Statsig's experiment setup runs this power analysis for you, but the shape is always the same: pick the effect size and the traffic, and the duration falls out. If it comes back longer than you can wait, you either chase a bigger effect, accept lower confidence, or run it on a higher-traffic surface.
