Explained in non-technical terms
Let's say I want to know if a new onboarding flow actually helps. If I only turn it on for people who already come back every day, of course it looks like a win, they were already coming back. A coin flip is what makes the two groups look the same before either one ever sees the feature.

A gate rollout can tell you the feature didn't break anything for the users you targeted. Only a group built by random assignment can tell you the feature caused the lift you're seeing.
