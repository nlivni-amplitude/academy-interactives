Pick a mode. Watch where the crunching happens and what leaves the building.
Cloud is sending your groceries to the vendor's kitchen: they keep a copy, cook the analysis, and plate the result. Warehouse-native is the chef coming to your kitchen instead. The recipes, SQL queries, travel to your warehouse, the cooking happens next to your own fridge, and only the finished tasting notes leave. Your groceries never go anywhere, and your fridge holds ingredients the vendor's kitchen never had: refunds, margins, offline orders.

At AmpliStore the CFO won't trust revenue from SDK purchase events, because real net revenue lives in Snowflake after refunds and fees, and legal won't let raw orders leave. Warehouse-native squares it: the SDK still assigns variants, but the success metric is net revenue after returns, computed inside Snowflake from the same tables the board sees. The trade is that results refresh on a schedule instead of in real time.
