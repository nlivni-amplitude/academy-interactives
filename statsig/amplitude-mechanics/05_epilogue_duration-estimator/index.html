Explained in non-technical terms
It's a fuel check before a road trip. How far is the destination, the smaller the lift you're chasing the farther away it lives. How fast is your car, that's traffic per day. The clever part is it doesn't ask you to guess your speed, it reads your actual fuel history, the last month of real traffic and conversion from your analytics. Some destinations come back 600 days away, and those trips you don't take.

At AmpliStore this triages the backlog. A hero-banner redesign expecting an 8% lift comes back about 7 weeks, so run it. A CTA copy tweak at 5% is about 4 months, viable only for a directional read at a lower confidence. A footer link color at 2% comes back over 600 days, so it's deleted from the backlog. That's the real job, not predicting the result but deciding which tests are worth building. Fine print: it uses t-tests only, it's a pre-launch estimate separate from the in-flight number on a running experiment, and its accuracy depends on the proxy event you pick and on traffic staying representative.
