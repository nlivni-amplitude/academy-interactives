Explained in non-technical terms
You sold lemonade on one corner and made six dollars. The Impact tab says every corner in town would make you 120. Maybe, but you haven't made 120, you've made six. It's a forecast built by scaling the observed effect up to full traffic, not a record of conversions that happened.

The safe sentence: "the Impact tab projects 50,000 subscriptions if we ship to 100%." The unsafe one: "our experiment proved we can add 50,000." The math is valid, the confidence is extrapolated. When a ratio metric shows an infinite relative delta, same spirit: the denominator's interval crosses zero, so the ratio blows up as a display edge case. Read the absolute per-user number instead.
