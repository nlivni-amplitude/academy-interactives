Two words get mixed up here. Statistically significant means the confidence interval excludes zero, so the effect is real. Practically significant means it's big enough to be worth shipping. A real +0.1% lift can clear the first bar and fail the second. You want both before you celebrate.

The trap in the error-rate row: its confidence interval also excludes zero, so the guardrail moved for real. A win on the primary doesn't cancel a real regression on a guardrail, they're both true at once, and both belong in the decision.
