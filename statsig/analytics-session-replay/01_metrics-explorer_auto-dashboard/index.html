Explained in non-technical terms
It's a photo album that files itself. Any photo you label "beach" jumps into the beach album on its own. You never glue photos in again, you just have to agree with everyone what the labels are. The one-time cost is settling on a tag taxonomy, checkout, onboarding, payments, and after that the dashboard maintains itself.

Building a dashboard by adding charts one at a time is doing it the hard way. Tag-based auto-populate adds a snapshot card for every new gate or experiment that carries a matching tag. This is a genuine Statsig-side difference: Amplitude dashboards are curated, you add charts or let a template scaffold them, with no direct tag-driven auto-populate for flags and experiments. Worth knowing when someone asks what's different about Statsig's dashboards.
