A trigger set to record isn't enough on its own. Recording passes through nested controls, and the outermost one has the final say. Toggle the conditions and check whether the session gets captured.

Global Targeting Gate
Who may ever be recorded. Excludes PII-sensitive segments and internal environments.
Global Sampling Rate
What share of eligible sessions record from the start. Defaults to 100%.
Conditional trigger
Start recording on this specific exposure or event.
Russian dolls. The outer doll, the targeting gate, decides who's even allowed in the game. If someone sits outside that outer doll, it doesn't matter how loudly the inner doll, the trigger, shouts "record," nothing happens. The trigger only gets to act on sessions the gate already let through.

When a trigger is set but zero sessions record, check in order: is the segment excluded by the Global Targeting Gate, is Session Replay enabled for the project, is the "all exposures" trigger being used (that needs a recent SDK), and has traffic actually hit the trigger yet. Amplitude's Session Replay matches on capability, the difference is the anchor: Statsig triggers off gate and experiment exposures, while Amplitude integrates with analytics charts so you jump from a funnel into the replays of users who dropped.
