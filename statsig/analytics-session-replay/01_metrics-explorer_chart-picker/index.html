Explained in non-technical terms
Metrics Explorer is a toolbox. Funnels ask where they stopped on the slide, retention asks whether they came back to the playground, distribution asks how many scoops everyone usually gets, drilldown is the magnifying glass that slices one metric by time and segment and source until one slice explains the whole move, and lifecycle asks whether these are new kids or the same kids coming back.

These map closely to Amplitude's Funnel Analysis, Retention, Distribution, Segmentation, and User Composition. The difference is that Statsig's versions are gate and experiment aware by default, so grouping by experiment group is first-class, while Amplitude Analytics goes broader and deeper for product-wide behavior. The rule of thumb: "did my gate or experiment cause this" starts in Statsig, "how do users behave across the whole product" starts in Amplitude.
