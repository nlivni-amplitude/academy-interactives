Seven easy hoops in a row and most of the class never gets the prize, and you can't tell which hoop was the annoying one, because they all leaked. The fix is to build funnels from three to five real decision points, the moments a user actually chooses to continue, not from every page load along the way.

At 80% per step a seven-step funnel ends around a quarter of where it started, and with seven candidate steps no single one stands out as the culprit. Keeping it to a few decision events means a real drop shows up as a sharp break at one step instead of a slow bleed across all of them.
