Think of three doors down a hallway, checked in order. Door 1's bouncer has a list, and even if you're on it he still runs a coin flip. Miss the list or lose the flip and you walk to Door 2. Nobody ever walks back to retry Door 1. Failing a rule for any reason, wrong targeting or lost lottery, drops you to the next one.

This is also where the AND-vs-OR surprise lives: two conditions inside one rule both have to be true, but two separate rules give you "either one" for free through this same fall-through.
