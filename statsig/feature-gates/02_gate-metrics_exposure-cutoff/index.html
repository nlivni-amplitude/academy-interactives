Your warehouse export stops receiving exposure events and the first instinct is a broken pipeline. Check the rollout percentage before opening an incident, because the gate stops logging on purpose at the two ends.

Explained in non-technical terms
A referee only keeps score while two teams are on the field. When one side has everybody, 100%, or nobody, 0%, there's no contest to score, so the whistle goes down. The gate does the same thing: with no split there's no comparison to record, so it stops emitting exposure events. Silence at the ends means the game ended, not that the whistle broke.

This is the first of three diagnostics checks when a gate looks off: is it being evaluated at all, does the pass rate match the rollout you set, and are passing users actually firing the metric event. A quiet log at 0% or 100% clears the first check without any fix.
