Your gate has two rules: internal users at 100%, everyone else at 50%. It's been live two weeks and you open the metrics expecting two lift numbers. You get one. Nothing's broken.

Rule 1 · Internal users · 100%
No lift shown. Everyone in this rule passes, so there's no fail group to compare them against. Not broken, there's just nothing to measure a difference against.
Rule 2 · Everyone else · 50%
You can't tell whether a vitamin works if every kid takes it. You need some kids who didn't, to compare against. A rule at 100% is every kid taking the vitamin, so lift has nothing to sit next to. Only a rule that splits its users, some passing and some failing, produces a comparison.

Drag Rule 2 to 0% or 100% and its panel goes quiet too, for the same reason. This is also why an empty panel at 50% isn't a bug: lift only appears once passing users have actually fired the metric event. No purchases yet means no purchase lift yet, not a broken gate.
