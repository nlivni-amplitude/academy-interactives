How many people completed checkout?
Each user is counted once per day, no matter how many times they checked out. A power user who completed checkout 4 times on Tuesday still counts as 1 for that day.
Watch for this: Switch to a bar chart in Amplitude and the same user is deduplicated across the entire date range — not per day. Your Unique Users number will drop. It's not a bug; line and bar charts count differently.
✨ Try it out: Open Event Segmentation, pick any event, and confirm the metric is set to Unique Users. Switch to a bar chart — does the number change?
